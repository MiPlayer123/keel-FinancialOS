# Code of Conduct

KEEL welcomes respectful participation from people of every background and
experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Never post credentials, financial records, private
  correspondence, or identifying screenshots.
- Assume good intent while taking security and financial-correctness concerns
  seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary, non-sensitive conduct concerns, use a repository issue. Do not
post sensitive or identifying information: a private reporting channel is not
enabled yet.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
