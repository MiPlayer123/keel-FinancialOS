## Information architecture
- A persistent top bar appears in every full-screen (non-modal) view with: leading icon(s), a centered contextual title, and a trailing "···" (more/options) circular button — seen in Reflect (ipad-01), Budget (ipad-02/03/05/06), and Home (ipad-07/08).
- Budget screens (ipad-02, 03, 05, 06) share one top bar: a pencil/edit-list icon and a hamburger/filter icon on the left, a centered "Aug 2025 ⌄" month-picker dropdown, and "···" on the right.
- Home screens (ipad-07, 08) share a different top bar: centered "Home" title, "···" top-right; ipad-07 additionally shows an "Assign" pill button top-right (between title and "···").
- Bottom navigation bar is only partially visible (ipad-07, cut off): icons with labels "Accounts" and "Reflect" are legible, implying a persistent tab bar with at least Budget/Accounts/Reflect destinations reachable in one tap from Home. A floating "+ Transaction" button sits bottom-right independent of the tab bar (visible in ipad-06 is absent, but present in ipad-05's underlying screen is not shown; confirmed present in ipad-03 modal and ipad-07/ipad-08 full screens).
- Reflect is grouped as a set of independent widget "cards" (Spending Breakdown, Income vs. Spending, Age of Money, Net Worth), each with its own leading icon, title, and trailing "›" chevron — implying each card is a one-tap drill-in to a fuller report (ipad-01).
- Budget categories are organized in two-level hierarchy: category group (e.g., "Credit Card Payments", "Living Expenses", "Irregular Expenses", "Savings", "Quality of Life") containing individual categories (e.g., "Mortgage", "Groceries", "Hawaii Vacation"). Groups are collapsible (chevron indicator) and each group row carries its own subtotal (Assigned/Activity/Available) distinct from the member rows (ipad-06).
- Tapping a category's amount pill from the Budget table appears to open a per-category detail modal (Balance + Target), as shown for "Hawaii Vacation" in ipad-03 — one tap from the budget list to full category detail.
- Home screen surfaces a condensed "Top Priorities" subset of goal-funded categories (Mortgage, Hawaii Vacation, Date Nights, Ice Skating Lessons) plus an "August Summary" card grid (Total Targets, Underfunded, Assigned, Spent), each summary card carrying its own "Edit ›" / "Reflect ›" / "Assign ›" deep link (ipad-07).
- Home screen also surfaces a "For You" educational content shelf ("See More" link, horizontally implied carousel via a "›" arrow on the card) with lesson-style cards, e.g. "YNAB Foundations" (ipad-08).
- Sharing/collaboration ("YNAB Together") and goal "Cover overspending" are both modal overlays launched from the Budget table, not separate top-level nav destinations (ipad-02, ipad-05).

## Flow steps
1. User is on the Budget table with an overspent category (ipad-05 background: "1 Oversent category" badge visible, "Cover" pill button present) → sees a red "1" count badge signaling at least one overspent category.
2. User taps into the overspending flow → sees "Cover Overspending" modal: category "🚗 Car Repair" shown with amount "−$300.00" on a red/salmon banner, and a "+ Select Category" button below it (ipad-05).
3. User taps "+ Select Category" → a bottom sheet slides up: "Cover overspending from" heading, a "Search Categories" field, and a list of eligible source categories grouped by their budget group ("Irregular Expenses" → Next iPhone $57.08; "Savings" → Hawaii Vacation $4,440.00, Emergency Savings $1,992.08), each with a green available-amount pill as the selectable control (ipad-05). No confirmation/undo screen is captured beyond this point — the flow is cut off here in the evidence.
4. Separately, from the Budget table, tapping a funded category (e.g. "Hawaii Vacation") opens its detail modal showing "Balance" (From Jul $3,500.00 → Assigned for Aug $1,500.00 → Activity in Aug −$560.00 → Available $4,440.00) stacked as a running ledger, followed by a "Target" section with a green checkmark and "You've met your target!" confirmation banner, then "Fill Up to $5,000.00 / By Sep 25 2025" and a "+ Transaction" action (ipad-03). This reads as a linear do→see sequence: assign money → spend against it → system computes availability → system evaluates against the goal target → confirms achievement.
5. Sharing: tapping into "YNAB Together" surfaces a role-grouped member list ("Group Manager": Toni (you); "Members": Ralph, Sarah) each row presumably drilling one level deeper into per-member permissions (chevron present, destination not captured) (ipad-02).

No explicit "undo" affordance is visible in any captured screen (see Open questions).

## States
- **Category funding states** (Budget table progress-bar captions, verbatim): "Funded", "Fully Spent", "Funded. Spent $572.17 of $800.00" (ipad-06); "On Track" (group-level, Irregular Expenses) and "On Track. Spent $560.00 of $5,000.00" (Hawaii Vacation category) (ipad-03).
- **Goal target success state**: green checkmark icon + banner copy "You've met your target!" (ipad-03) — an explicit success/celebration state tied to a specific numeric condition (available ≥ target).
- **Plain-language interpretive state** (Reflect, Income vs. Spending card): "So far, you're spending less than you make." — a natural-language status synthesized from the underlying Income/Spending comparison, not a raw number (ipad-01).
- **Overspending state**: red/salmon-colored banner + "Oversent category" count row + red numeric badge "1", framed as a warning/negative-money state (color = red per negative money) (ipad-05).
- **Zero-value / neutral state**: "$0.00" rendered in a flat gray pill (visually distinct from the green "available" pill), e.g. Household Items after being "Fully Spent" (ipad-06); "Ice Skating Lessons" row on Home shows no pill at all, which may itself be a distinct "no target/unfunded" state (unconfirmed, see Open questions).
- No loading, error, or empty-state screens are present anywhere in this unit's evidence — all 8 images show populated, successful states (expected, since these are polished marketing screenshots, not raw app captures).

## Business rules implied
- A category's "Available" figure is computed as Assigned + Activity (running arithmetic) and can be negative, shown as plain red/muted "−$" text rather than a pill, distinct from the pill treatment for non-negative availability (ipad-06, ipad-05 background: Household Items "−$127.16" Activity next to "$0.00" Available pill).
- Category groups carry their own subtotals independent of, but derived from, member categories (Assigned/Activity/Available rollups shown at the group-header row) — implies budget totals are hierarchical/aggregating, not flat (ipad-06).
- Overspending in one category can be "covered" by reassigning available funds from another category or a savings/goal category (Hawaii Vacation, Emergency Savings appear as valid cover sources) — implies category-to-category fund transfer is a first-class, explicit user action requiring category selection, not automatic (ipad-05).
- A goal/target category tracks a running "Balance" ledger across months (From Jul → Assigned for Aug → Activity in Aug → Available) rather than a single point-in-time total, implying goal balances persist and roll forward month over month (ipad-03).
- Goals have both a dollar target and a date target evaluated jointly ("Fill Up to $5,000.00" / "By Sep 25 2025"), and system explicitly flags when the dollar target is met independent of the date ("You've met your target!" shown while a future date target still remains) (ipad-03).
- Sharing/collaboration is role-based with at least two tiers: "Group Manager" (singular, "(you)" in this account) and "Members" (plural, multiple allowed) — implies a permission hierarchy rather than flat equal access (ipad-02).
- "Ready to Assign" is tracked as a distinct pool of money separate from category assignments, displayed prominently at both the top of the Budget table and on the Home screen — implies unassigned funds are a first-class state in the budgeting model, not implicitly zero (ipad-02/03/05/06 and ipad-07).

## Standout details
- Category funding-bar fill pattern doubles as micro-status encoding: a fully solid bar = "Funded", a hatched/diagonal-striped bar = "Fully Spent" — same visual affordance (a bar) but a different texture communicates a materially different state (money available vs. money exhausted) without needing to read the caption text (ipad-06).
- The "Available" amount is never just a number — it is always wrapped in a colored pill (green = positive, gray = zero), making polarity scannable across a dense list without reading every value (ipad-06, ipad-05, ipad-07).
- Report cards on Reflect lead with a plain-English interpretive sentence before showing the chart ("So far, you're spending less than you make.") — translates a two-series comparison into a single, non-numeric verdict a user can grasp instantly (ipad-01).
- The goal detail modal explicitly separates "Balance" (what happened) from "Target" (what's the goal) as two distinct labeled sections in the same panel, and shows a checkmark + affirming copy the moment the numeric condition is met, rather than only showing progress bars (ipad-03).
- Home screen's stat-grid cards each carry their own contextual deep link matching the metric's home surface ("Total Targets" → "Edit ›", "Underfunded" → "Reflect ›", "Assigned" → "Assign ›") — the destination is tailored per metric rather than one generic "See more" (ipad-07).
- The "For You" content shelf embeds bite-sized financial education directly into the primary Home surface, with a lightweight time/format affordance ("4 min · Read") that sets expectations before tapping (ipad-08).
- A persistent floating "+ Transaction" button appears to follow the user across contexts — visible atop a Budget-triggered modal (ipad-03) and on the Home screen (ipad-07, ipad-08) — suggesting transaction entry is globally one tap away regardless of current screen.
- "Cost to Be Me" is used as the literal product label for total monthly required spending (Edit Plan screen headline), a distinctive, non-generic naming choice for what is otherwise a "total budgeted" figure (ipad-04).
