- "Reports" has its own secondary tab bar, separate from the left rail: observed tabs across captures are Home, Income Summary, Spending Summary, Spending, Income, "2026 YTD Income" (a user-saved/pinned report, marked with a bookmark icon), Income & Expense, Net Worth, Taxes. Some tabs carry a small orange dot badge (seen on "Spending" in `community-05` and `community-07`) while the currently-active, unmodified tab ("Income" in `community-06`) does not.
- A "Transaction Activity" (or "Income/Expense transactions by category") table is embedded as a sub-panel on at least four different surfaces — single-account detail, spending-watchlist category detail, and both Reports tabs — always below a chart/summary block, suggesting the register/table is a reusable component rather than a page unto itself.
- One click away, per visible affordances: "See all Bills & Income" (from Dashboard's Bills & Income card) presumably opens a dedicated Bills & Income page (not captured in this unit); "+ New"/"+ Add" on the Accounts panel presumably opens account creation; the Reports gear icon opens the column/display-settings popover in place (not a new screen).

## Business rules implied
- Reminders/upcoming items carry a relative due-date badge (e.g. "tomorrow", "In 5 days", "on Apr 25") and a colored initial-avatar, consistently across the Spending Plan ("Past"/"Today"/"Upcoming" tags, `community-01`), Net Worth Reminders carousel (`community-02`), and account Cash Flow carousel (`community-03`) — implying a single underlying "bills/income reminder" object reused across surfaces with a shared date-relative-status vocabulary (Past / Today / Upcoming / "on <date>" / "in N days").
- Accounts can be grouped under a custom top-level entity/tag distinct from account type — evidenced by sibling groups "GREEN KITCHEN" (apparent business) and "PERSONAL", each independently subdivided into Banking/Investments/Assets/Liabilities (`community-02`, `community-08`).
- The Spending Plan enforces exactly five budget buckets per month — Income, Bills, Planned Spend, Other Spend, Goals — each with its own running total, from which a single derived "Left this month" / "$X left per day" figure is computed (`community-01`).
- A per-category numeric "Target" (e.g. `$150.00` for Groceries) can be set and is rendered as a fixed horizontal threshold line across a 12-month bar history, visually distinct from the monthly spend bars themselves (`community-04`).
- Recurring/scheduled transactions are flagged with a distinct repeat/loop icon in transaction rows (seen on an "Igc Web Payments" row in the register, `community-02`), separating recurring from one-off transactions at the row level.
- Reports can be saved as new persistent, named tabs — evidenced by "2026 YTD Income" appearing as its own top-level Reports tab with a bookmark icon alongside the generic "Income"/"Spending" tabs (`community-07`), reachable via the "Save" button.
- A report tab shows a small orange-dot badge when its live filter/config state differs from its saved default — "Spending" carries the dot in both `community-05` and `community-07` while the currently-open, unmodified "Income" tab does not in `community-06` — and correspondingly "Reset report" only appears once a report has been altered from default (present in `community-05`, absent in `community-06`).
- Transactions can be explicitly excluded from a spending total without being deleted — the Spending Plan's "Excluded this month (1)" disclosure and the Reports "Show excluded transactions" toggle both point to an include/exclude flag on transactions independent of their existence in the ledger (`community-01`, `community-07`).
- Table display preferences — sign convention ("Show expenses as negative"), decimal precision ("Hide cents"), inclusion of excluded rows, column set, row height, and alternate-row shading — are user-configurable per report view and are explicitly resettable via "Reset to defaults," implying these are persisted view-level settings distinct from the underlying transaction data (`community-07`).

- `community-06` and `community-07` contain externally-added magenta arrow/box annotations (pointing at the "Transaction" dropdown and the column-settings popover respectively) that are not part of Simplifi's native UI; they were treated as curator emphasis rather than product chrome and excluded from the Controls inventory, but they do mean this unit's evidence was pre-filtered by whoever selected/annotated these two images toward those specific controls.
- Whether "Transaction" and "Category" in the Reports toolbar are simple dropdowns, mode toggles, or something else is inferred only from a small caret glyph — no expanded/open state of either control was captured.
- The precise semantics of the colored initial-avatars (e.g., whether "I" denotes a specific merchant "Idaho Power" vs. a broader "utility/institution" category) is inferred from repeated payee association across images, not confirmed by any visible legend or label.
- Whether the unsigned, `$`-less amounts in transaction-register and report-table rows (e.g. `20.81`, `43.92`) always represent expenses/debits, or whether credits would appear with an explicit `+`/color elsewhere off-frame, cannot be fully confirmed since no credit line item happened to appear within any visible table row in this unit (the only credited/positive amounts observed are in card/reminder contexts, not table rows).
