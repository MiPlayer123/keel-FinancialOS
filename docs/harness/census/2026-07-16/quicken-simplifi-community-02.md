## Business rules implied
- A rule's IF clause can combine independently-toggleable conditions — Payee, Account, Amount — each with its own enable checkbox, meaning conditions are AND-composed only when checked (community-x01's row 1 shows all three combined: statement-name-contains, specific account, amount range).
- Payee matching can target either the raw bank-supplied text ("Original statement name") or Simplifi's own cleaned name ("Quicken name"), with "Original statement name" called out as "Recommended" (community-x02/x03/x04) — implying the product steers users toward matching on raw, stable statement text rather than a name that itself might be rule-modified.
- Keyword matching under "Contains" requires ALL listed keywords to be present ("All keywords must be present for a rule to apply") and supports "OR conditions to target different keyword combinations" as a separate mechanism, with "Commas or spaces enter a new keyword" as the input convention (community-x03/x04).
- Amount conditions can be scoped by transaction sign/type and a numeric range simultaneously, per the rules-list prose "Amount is an Expense between $30 and $40" (community-x01) — i.e., the Amount condition rule model is (direction: Expense/Income) × (min, max), not just a bare range.
- Category assignment in a rule can be split by account usage — one category for business accounts, a different one for personal accounts of the same matched payee — via "Categorize for business and personal accounts separately" (community-x02/x03/x04), and this is explicitly the "Recommended" configuration (community-x03), implying business/personal separation is a first-class, encouraged modeling concept, not an edge case.
- Applying a new/edited rule is understood to potentially affect transactions that already exist, not just future ones — the wizard footer explicitly warns "You'll handle existing transactions next" before advancing (community-x02/x03/x04), i.e., rule creation and historical backfill are deliberately sequenced as separate decisions.
- Rules ordering/precision supports two distinct match strategies for the same payee (community-x01): a compound conditional rule (contains "wal"+"mart" AND account AND amount range) versus a stricter, unconditioned exact-match rule ("is exactly 'Walmart'"), each producing different category outcomes — implying rule evaluation must have some precedence/first-match-wins or all-match logic to reconcile overlapping rules, though the exact precedence order is not evidenced.
- Splits must fully account for the parent transaction's total before completion is implied by the persistent "LEFT TO SPLIT" running-balance readout (community-09) — it is presented as a live remainder rather than a static field, implying the total is validated against zero (not directly confirmed as a hard block, since Continue's enabled/disabled state at nonzero-remaining is not shown).
- A transaction can be flagged to exclude itself from downstream calculations at two independent granularities — "Spending Plan" and "Reports" (which itself further affects "Watchlists" and "Upcoming Summary") — meaning "exclude from budgeting" and "exclude from reporting/analytics" are modeled as separate booleans, not one blanket exclude flag (community-x05/x06).
- A transaction can be marked "Billable" and tied to a "Clients & Projects" record independently of category/tags, implying a lightweight project/client-billing layer exists alongside normal categorization (community-x05/x06).
- Bills & Income reminders carry a per-instance paid/unpaid status independent of due date proximity — an item due "today" can already be "Paid" while a same-payee item due "tomorrow" is not (community-x07), so "Paid" is not simply inferred from date-has-passed.
- The Summary card's Income/Expenses/Net figures are computed over a caller-selected window ("Next 30 Days", resolved to "Jan 30, 2025 - Mar 1, 2025") and explicitly exclude any reminder the user has hidden, with the exclusion count surfaced rather than silently applied (community-x07): 7,950.14 − 1,031.01 = 6,919.13, confirming Net = Income − Expenses exactly as displayed.

## Standout details
- The rules-list "If … / Then …" table (community-x01) renders rule logic as plain, readable prose with inline emphasis (bold condition values, blue-linked category/rename targets) rather than a raw condition-builder summary — a rule like "Original statement name contains wal mart / Account is Intuit Checking / Amount is an Expense between $30 and $40 → Rename as Walmart Personal, Update Category to Groceries" reads like a sentence, not a spec dump.
- The rule wizard surfaces a contextual coaching banner ("Now, add THEN conditions that you want your rule to change.") with a hand-drawn-style bent arrow pointing at the exact panel it refers to, rather than a generic tooltip — directional, in-context onboarding (community-x02).

- The Split Transaction footer distinguishes "Delete all splits" (undo the split entirely, back to one line) from "Cancel" (abandon this editing session) — two different destructive/exit intents given separate, clearly-labeled controls instead of overloading one "Cancel" (community-09).
- Exclude toggles in Transaction detail are paired with meaningfully different icons — a percent-with-slash glyph for "Spending Plan" vs an eye-with-slash glyph for "Reports" — reinforcing that one hides the transaction from budget math while the other hides it from visibility/analytics, purely via iconography (community-x05).
- The Bills & Income calendar uses small dot markers (not numbers) under dates with scheduled items, keeping the calendar grid visually quiet, with the current/selected day distinguished by an outline rather than a fill — a restrained way to surface density without clutter (community-x07).
- "1 Reminders are excluded" is stated as a caveat directly beneath the headline Income/Expenses/Net numbers, with the eye-slash icon reused from the Transaction-detail "Reports" exclude toggle (community-x05 vs x07) — suggesting a consistent icon vocabulary for "hidden from this view" across at least two different surfaces.
