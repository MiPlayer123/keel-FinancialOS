## Business rules implied
- A recurring merchant match is governed by explicit, user/system-visible rule parameters: a name substring match, an amount tolerance range, a day-of-month constraint, and a cadence — evidenced by the "RULES" chips in `community-09-mac-recurrings.png` ("Named **Property Payment Rent Ca**", "from **$1,388** to **$2,835**", "on **any day of the month**", "every **month**"). This implies recurring detection tolerates amount variance within a bounded range rather than requiring an exact match.
- "Overdue" recurring items still display a positive completion checkmark alongside the "Overdue" label in `community-09-mac-recurrings.png`, suggesting overdue-but-since-paid items retain a "confirmed occurred" mark distinct from a pure due/undue flag.
- Percent-change coloring is tied to favorability of the metric, not literal sign/direction alone: green=favorable/red=unfavorable holds for balances (`community-11-mac-accounts.png`, `community-10-mac-dashboard-dark.png`) AND for spend increases shown in red despite an up-arrow (`community-x05-cashflow-spend-category-breakdown.png`), evidencing that the color encodes "good vs. bad for the user" rather than "increase vs. decrease."
- Goals that exceed their target amount are not automatically marked "complete" with a special state — they instead appear filed under different lifecycle sections ("Ready to spend" for Dream Wedding at $16,680/$10,000; "Archived" for Pet Care Fund at $251/$50) — evidenced in `community-x02-goals-list-active-ready-archived.png` — implying goal lifecycle (Active/Ready to spend/Archived) is a separate, likely user-driven or rule-driven status independent of raw progress-ratio math.
- Percentage-change display appears clamped at a sentinel ("999%") when the comparison baseline is at or near zero, rather than showing an unbounded or undefined value — evidenced by `community-x05-cashflow-spend-category-breakdown.png` ("↗999% vs $0.00 in Jan 2023").
