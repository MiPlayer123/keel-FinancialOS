## Business rules implied
- Budgets are monthly and per-category; spending against a category is tracked whether or not a budget amount has been set (`budgets-desktop.png`: every row shows "$X spent" even with no target, footer copy confirms this explicitly).
- Budgets exclude transfers from spending tracked against a category (`budgets-desktop.png` subtitle: "spending tracked against it, transfers excluded" — consistent with Reports' repeated "confirmed transfers excluded" footnotes).
- A "Copy last month" action exists for budgets, implying budgets are not automatically rolled forward month-to-month by default (`budgets-desktop.png`).
- Goals are described as non-transactional earmarking — "nothing moves, it's just spoken for" (`goals-desktop.png` subtitle) — i.e., a goal is a soft allocation label, not a fund transfer or sub-account, consistent with Law 1 (LLMs/system never do ledger arithmetic beyond deterministic tracking) and the "suggest" framing elsewhere.
- Paycheck recording enforces an arithmetic identity: gross − deductions = net = deposit, and reconciles that net against the real bank deposit (`paychecks-desktop.png` subtitle and CTA-adjacent copy: "KEEL checks the math (gross – deductions = net = deposit) and ties it to the real bank deposit") — a concrete, evidenced instance of Law 1's "deterministic spine" and Law 9's reproducibility.
- Recurring-bill/subscription detection is automatic, runs on a nightly batch cadence, and requires human confirmation before being treated as a rule ("KEEL detects... and suggests them here for your approval. Detection runs nightly." — `recurring-desktop.png`) — consistent with Law 2 (suggest→approve) and the AI risk ladder's Class B treatment of rules.
- Recurring items, once confirmed, project into a "balance curve" and can be one-click entered into the ledger on their due date (`recurring-desktop.png`: "KEEL shows it as due, projects it into your balance curve, and one click enters it into the ledger on the day.") — implies a forecast/projection view (Class C preview) distinct from the actual ledger write (which requires the one-click action, i.e., approval).
- Reports figures are consistently scoped and disclosed: every report widget carries an explicit as-of/scope footnote (date range, "dominant currency only," "confirmed transfers excluded," "net of refunds") — direct evidence of Law 9's reproducible-numbers invariant being implemented in the UI copy itself (`reports-desktop.png`, multiple footnotes).
- Raw bank-memo text is surfaced verbatim in a report ("Biggest single purchase: Online Payment 297756436872 To CITIBANK CREDIT CARD 06/25") without transformation — consistent with Law 5 (ingested text is data-tier, displayed but not acted upon) and Law 9 (source preservation).
- The current month in Reports is explicitly marked incomplete/in-progress ("the current month is still in progress" — `reports-desktop.png` footnote on the This-month-vs-last-month card), guarding against mis-reading a partial month as a completed comparison.
- Category rows in the Reports 6-month table are clickable and route to a filtered Ledger view ("Click a category to open it in the ledger" — `reports-desktop.png`), evidencing a designed cross-surface link between Reports and Ledger.

## Standout details
- Every Reports widget ends in a plain-language scope/exclusion footnote rather than an icon or tooltip — a deliberate "reproducible numbers" pattern (matches Law 9) done via visible micro-copy instead of hidden metadata, which is unusually disciplined and worth preserving as a pattern across all future report surfaces.
- The Sankey diagram's flow labels double as a legend (e.g. "Income · $10,260.01" directly on the node) rather than requiring a separate color-key lookup — reduces cognitive load versus a chart+separate-legend split.
- Paychecks subtitle states the exact reconciliation formula in plain English ("gross – deductions = net = deposit") right in the empty-state CTA copy, teaching the user the mental model before they've entered any data — a nice piece of progressive disclosure/onboarding-via-microcopy.
- The "This month vs last month" report block uses directional arrow glyphs (↘/↗) paired with color to encode increase/decrease per category, adjacent to a from→to value pair, so the user gets magnitude, direction, and absolute before/after in one compact row.
- Recurring page explicitly tells the user the batch cadence of the detection job ("Detection runs nightly") — an operational transparency detail rarely surfaced this plainly in competitor products' empty states.
- Money figures throughout use a widened decimal ("$679 . 40"-style spacing) that gives a subtle tabular-figure/monospace rhythm to number columns even in a proportional UI font — worth noting as a typographic micro-choice for number legibility.
