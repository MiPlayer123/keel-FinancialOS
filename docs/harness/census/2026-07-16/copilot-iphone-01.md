## Business rules implied
- A budget-left figure is computed against a stated total ("$477 left" / "out of $4,120 budgeted") and rendered with a pacing chart comparing actual cumulative spend against a projected/reference line, with a labeled delta ("$268 under") — evidencing that the app computes and surfaces a "pace vs. plan" figure, not just a running total (image 02).
- Transactions can be marked reviewed in bulk from a "So far today" grouping via a single "MARK AS REVIEWED" action, implying review status is a per-transaction flag settable in batch for a time-grouped set (image 02).
- Each spend category carries a persistent color+emoji identity reused across contexts — e.g., Groceries is green/🥑 in both the Dashboard ring row (image 02) and the standalone widget card (image 05); Restaurants is orange/🍔 in both the To-review row (image 02) and the Budget-review bar (image 03) — evidencing a stable per-category visual identity used for fast scanning across surfaces.
- Budget-vs-actual can be displayed in two units (Amount vs Percentage) via a single toggle without navigating away, implying both are pre-computed and always available (image 03).
- A single "TARGET" threshold line is drawn across all category bars simultaneously in the Budget review screen, implying budget target is evaluated per-category but visualized against one shared marker for at-a-glance over/under comparison (image 03).
- Recurring bills carry an explicit due day-of-month ("1st", "4th", "5th", "15th") and a paid/confirmed marker (colored checkmark badge), and are bucketed into "This month" vs "In the future" — implying the system distinguishes current-period recurring obligations from later-dated ones rather than listing all on one flat timeline (images 04/06).
- Income and spend trends are each shown with a same-period-last-year comparison and a directional percentage badge, and the badge's color polarity is metric-dependent: rising income is colored green/positive ("↗ 23%"), rising spend is colored red/negative ("↗ 34%") despite both being arrows pointing up — evidencing that "up" is not universally good/bad, the app assigns color by whether the underlying metric increasing is favorable (image 07).
- The cash-flow bar chart's y-axis floor is set to the actual minimum data value ("-$762") rather than a round number, implying the chart auto-scales its axis to the data range rather than using fixed round gridlines (image 07).
- A transaction detail exposes both a category assignment and the originating funding account/card as adjacent, separately-tappable elements, implying category and account/card are tracked as distinct, independently editable attributes of a transaction (image 08).

## Standout details
- The Dashboard pace chart's use of a dashed straight reference line plus a solid gradient (yellow→green) actual line, with a single inline pill-tooltip anchored at the current point ("$268 under") rather than a full axis/legend — a very low-chrome way to answer "am I on track" at a glance (image 02).
- Color-polarity flips per metric on the same chart type: an "↗" (up arrow) badge is green for net income but red for spend, in near-identical card layouts placed back-to-back (image 07) — a subtle but important convention (arrow direction ≠ value judgment; color carries the judgment).
- Category identity (emoji + color) is treated as a reusable token consistently paired across at least three different surfaces (ring row, budget bars, widget cards, transaction pills) rather than re-derived per screen.
- Two distinct visual encodings are used for "budget remaining" depending on context: circular progress rings for a compact multi-category glance (image 02), versus horizontal bars against one shared dashed target line for a detailed monthly review (image 03) — different chart forms chosen for skim vs. compare tasks.
- The account-mask convention on the widget card shows only the last 4 digits ("2124") next to the account nickname ("Checking"), balancing recognizability with privacy (image 05).
- The support hub folds a feedback-collection prompt ("What feature do you want to see next?") directly into the help/contact surface rather than a separate feedback form, and sets an explicit response-time expectation inline ("We typically reply within a day") right on the CTA (image 01).
- The Recurrings "paid so far" vs. "left to pay" split is visualized with one shared ring rather than two separate numbers with no visual tie, giving an immediate sense of the month's completion ratio (images 04/06).
- The light-mode/dark-mode pair (images 04 vs 06) is pixel-identical in layout and content, confirming theming is a straight color-token swap with no content or density changes between modes.
