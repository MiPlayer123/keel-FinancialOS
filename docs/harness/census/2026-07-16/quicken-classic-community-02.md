## Business rules implied
- Net worth is defined as the sum across all account groups, per the explicit callout "Net Worth is total value of all accounts" (community-10), and the sidebar's own arithmetic is consistent with summing Banking + Investing + Property & Debt group totals down to the Net Worth row.
- Accounts are partitioned into exactly three top-level groups for net-worth purposes: `Banking`, `Investing`, `Property & Debt` (community-10) — investing/retirement accounts are tracked separately from cash/checking/savings/credit-card accounts ("Non-banking accounts are listed separately").
- Negative balances (debt: credit card, liability) are always rendered in red text, both in the sidebar and in group/net-worth rollups (community-10) — sign-as-color is applied at every level of the rollup, not just leaf accounts.
- A per-account "new transactions available" state exists and is visually distinct (blue dot) from the read/settled state (community-10).
- Budgets support a hierarchical category tree with parent categories that roll up children (`Home` and `Kids` both contain named sub-items, expand/collapse via `-`/`+`), i.e., budget categories are not flat (community-09).
- Budget bars carry a status color distinct from plain progress (red for `New Roof` vs. green for every other row) — implying some threshold/exception rule differentiates at least one category, though the exact rule is not legible from this image (community-09).
- Spending reports compute a category breakdown against a grand total, with each category expressed as both an absolute dollar figure and a percentage of that total, and a `Total` row that sums the category amounts (community-11: $4,450.00+$1,225.00+$798.00+$320.00+$250.00+$200.00+$45.00+$150.00 = $7,438.00, which does NOT equal the displayed Total of $7,118.00 — see Open questions).
- A security "what-if" sell calculates: Gross Proceeds = shares × average price; Pre-tax gain = Gross Proceeds − Cost Basis; After-Tax gain = Pre-tax gain − Estimated Federal Tax (community-12: 12,201.70 − 4,487.68 = 7,714.02 ✓; 7,714.02 − 1,157.00 = 6,557.02 ✓ — both check out arithmetically).
- The buy/sell preview is explicitly non-committal/exploratory ("Buy/Sell Preview", "What if I…") and offers a separate, more precise tool ("Capital Gains Estimator") for lot-specific control, implying the preview itself uses an average-cost-basis assumption rather than specific-lot selection (community-12).
