- **Copilot institution picker (7)**: presented as a centered modal/dialog (title "SELECT YOUR INSTITUTION", all-caps, gray) with a single X close, no back arrow, no progress bar — implying it is an entry point rather than a mid-wizard step, or that Copilot's own multi-step chrome isn't shown in this capture.

## Flow steps
1. Do: open account-linking (host app "WonderWallet") → See: consent screen with phone-number capture, "Continue" / "Continue as guest" options (image 1).
2. Do: tap Continue → See: institution search/grid screen, progress bar begins (segment 1–2 of 3 filled) (image 2).
3. Do: select an institution (e.g. "Gingham Bank") → See: pre-redirect disclosure screen naming the institution and stating exactly what data will be shared, with a "Continue to log in" CTA carrying an external-link glyph (image 3). Back arrow still available (can return to institution search).
4. Do: tap "Continue to log in" → See: handed off to the institution's own branded login page (no Plaid chrome, no back/close), pre-filled sandbox test credentials, "SIGN IN" button (image 4).
5. Do: sign in → See: "Select accounts" screen, progress bar now fully filled (3 of 3), back arrow no longer present (cannot return past this point in this capture), checkboxes for each discovered account with live balances, scoped consent copy naming both institution and app (image 5).
6. Do: select account(s) and tap "Continue" → See: terminal "Success!" screen confirming connection to the named app, single "Continue" button to dismiss (image 6).
- Undo/back affordances: back arrow present only on the institution-search and OAuth-handoff steps (images 2–3); absent from account-selection and success (images 5–6) — no visible in-flow undo once past the bank-credentials step. No explicit "cancel/undo the link" control anywhere in this sequence, only the X (which presumably abandons the whole flow, not surgically undoes a step).

## Business rules implied
- A phone number is collected for optional faster re-authentication ("Use your phone number to log in or sign up with Plaid to go faster next time"), but is not mandatory — a guest path exists ("Continue as guest") (image 1).
- Continuing past the consent screen implies agreement to Plaid's Terms and Privacy Policy — consent is bundled into the primary CTA, not a separate checkbox (image 1).
- Before redirecting to a bank's own login (OAuth-style), the user is shown an explicit, itemized disclosure of exactly what will be shared: "account and routing numbers, account and balance info, and contact details" (image 3) — data-sharing scope is disclosed per-institution before credential entry, not only at the end.
- Account selection is granular and per-account: the consent copy explicitly states data is shared only "from the Gingham Bank accounts you select," implying a user can link an institution but withhold specific accounts (e.g., share checking, not savings) (image 5).
- The linking flow names both the specific financial institution and the specific consuming app at every consent checkpoint (images 3, 5, 6), rather than using generic language — traceable, specific consent scope shown to the user at each step.
- In Copilot's own institution picker, a small curated set of "featured" institutions/services (including non-bank entries like Apple and Venmo) is shown ahead of the full Plaid-backed search, with a separate escape hatch "Link other institutions through Plaid" for anything not featured (image 7).
- Budget category rows compute and visually encode over/under status per row independently at both the group and leaf level (e.g., a group can show green while a child inside it shows red, as with Car & Transportation's green-ish parent bar vs. its red Transportation subcategory) (image 8) — rollup and per-leaf status are tracked and displayed at every level of the hierarchy, not just the leaf.
- "Left" (remaining budget) is computed and signed (positive when under, negative with a leading minus when over), and paired with a color dot rather than only text color, at every row of the bottom data table (image 8).

## Standout details
- The password field on the simulated bank login page has a biometric/Face-ID glyph embedded directly inside the input's trailing edge rather than as a separate button — a compact "alternate auth" affordance (image 4).
- "Continue to log in" carries an inline external-link icon glyph in the button label itself, visually pre-warning the user they are about to leave the current context before they click (image 3).
- The 3-segment progress bar uses a teal-to-blue color gradient across segments rather than a flat single-color fill, giving a sense of directional progress/motion (images 2, 3, 5).
- The account-selection consent sentence bolds both the institution name and the app name inline within an otherwise plain sentence, drawing the eye to exactly what's being scoped at the exact decision point (image 5).
- Copilot's "Rebalance" action sits as a first-class button directly beside "Categories," one click away from the whole list — implying an automated/AI-assisted budget-redistribution feature surfaced at the top of the primary management view rather than buried in settings (image 8).
- The "Key metrics" section header carries a "?" info icon beside it, suggesting an inline definitional tooltip for how each metric (e.g., "Average monthly spend") is computed, rather than requiring the user to guess or leave the page (image 8).
- Months with no historical data are rendered as a flat dashed line leading into the bar chart, visually distinct from a literal $0 bar — a deliberate "no data" vs. "zero spend" convention (image 8).
- The right pane's headline spend figure carries 2-decimal precision ("$3,673.10") while the visually adjacent left-pane summary tile rounds the same figure to whole dollars ("$3,673") — a deliberate precision split between glance and detail contexts within the same screen (image 8).
