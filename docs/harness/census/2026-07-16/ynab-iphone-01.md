## Information architecture
- No bottom tab bar is visible in any image (all cropped above/at the device chrome), so the full top-level nav structure cannot be enumerated directly. Inferred top-level areas from content: **Home**, a monthly **Budget** table (opened from a month header, e.g. "Aug 2025 ⌄"), a **Reflect** reports area (bar chart + net worth), **Goals** (individual goal detail screens), and a **Together** member-sharing area — plus a **Watch** companion app.
- Home (`iphone-03`, `iphone-07`) is the hub: it surfaces a transaction-review queue ("2 New transactions" → "Assign"/"Review"), the "Ready to Assign" figure with an "Edit" action, a collapsible "Top Priorities" category shortcut list, a collapsible "August Summary" section, and further down a "For You" educational-content feed with a "See More" link and a support CTA ("Connect With Our Team"). A global "+" floating button (bottom right) and a "…" overflow menu (top right) are present on Home.
- Budget (`iphone-05`) is one tap from the month indicator on Home ("Aug 2025 ⌄") and organizes categories into named, independently collapsible groups (e.g. "Credit Card Payments", "Living Expenses"), each with its own column header describing what the number means for that group ("Available for Payment" vs "Available to Spend") — i.e. IA groups categories by cash-flow role, not just alphabetically.
- Each individual category/goal (e.g. "Hawaii Vacation") has its own detail screen (`iphone-04`) reachable by drilling into a category/goal row from Home's Top Priorities list, the Budget table's Savings group, or the Cover-Overspending category picker — the same entity is surfaced in at least three different list contexts, each showing a different subset of its data (name+amount only in lists; full Balance/Target breakdown on the detail screen).
- "Cover Overspending" (`iphone-08`) is a modal sheet layered on top of budgeting; it opens a nested "Cover overspending from" sub-sheet with its own search field and grouped category picker (mirrors the Budget table's own group/row layout: "Regular Expenses", "Savings").
- "YNAB Together" (`iphone-01`) is presented as a full-screen modal (X-to-close, not a back chevron), suggesting it hangs off Settings/overflow rather than being a primary tab. It groups members by role: "Group Manager" (singular, the account owner) vs "Members" (plural, invitees).
- Watch app (`iphone-06`) mirrors the phone Budget IA at a glance: "Pinned" categories surface first, then "Bills" — a deliberately reduced, glanceable subset of the full budget grouping.

## Business rules implied
- Every category tracks at least three distinct figures across a period — "Assigned for Aug", "Activity in Aug", and "Available" — each independently drillable (chevron), and "Available" additionally carries forward a prior-period balance ("From Jul $3,500.00") before the current month's assignment/activity are applied. Evidenced by `iphone-04`'s Balance card.
- A category's funded state is computed as a function of assigned vs. spent, not a flat threshold: the same green "positive pill" styling is reused for $1,500.00 (fully funded, nothing spent yet) and $227.83 (partially spent, still positive available), while $0.00 available gets a visually different (non-green, hatched-bar) treatment even though it is not negative. Evidenced by `iphone-05`.
- Overspending a category surfaces as a distinct, separately-named workflow ("Cover Overspending") requiring the user to actively choose a source category with positive available balance to cover the gap — the negative balance is not silently absorbed or left unresolved. Evidenced by `iphone-08`.
- A goal (e.g., "Hawaii Vacation") is simultaneously (a) a budget category with a monthly Assigned/Activity/Available figure, and (b) a target with its own fill-amount and target-date, tracked and displayed as two separate cards on the same detail screen — goals are not a separate ledger from the monthly budget, they're the same category with extra target metadata layered on. Evidenced by `iphone-04` combined with the "Hawaii Vacation" row appearing plainly inside the Budget table's category list logic (Savings group) in `iphone-08`'s picker.
- Plan sharing has exactly two roles: a single "Group Manager" (the paying/owning account) and any number of "Members" — evidenced by the section grouping and singular vs. plural headers in `iphone-01`, with no third role/tier shown.
- The Home tab treats "new transactions needing review" as a first-class, numerically badged queue item separate from "Ready to Assign" — implying categorization/review and budget-assignment are two distinct required actions surfaced side-by-side rather than merged into one prompt. Evidenced by `iphone-03`.

## Standout details
- Progress bar + plain-English caption pairing on budget rows ("Funded. Spent $572.17 of $800.00") does the arithmetic in words directly under the number — no mental math needed to know how much of the category is left versus the raw dollar figure alone.
- Distinguishing "$0.00, fully spent" (hatched bar, gray pill) from "$0.00, not yet touched" is a state most budgeting UIs collapse into one — this one visibly does not, at least for the "Fully Spent" case shown.
- The overspend pill uses a soft pink rather than alarm-red for the number itself, reserving the strongest red for the section background/label ("Debts" text, panel chrome) — i.e., red-intensity appears to scale with role (structural warning vs. a specific figure) rather than being applied uniformly to "any negative number."
- "Ready to Assign" is rendered as the single largest, highest-contrast element on the Budget screen (full-width green pill spanning the row) — it is the one number the screen wants noticed first, ahead of any category detail.
- The Home tab explicitly separates two distinct required user actions each with their own count/amount and CTA verb ("2 New transactions → Assign" vs "$1,000.00 Ready to assign → Edit") rather than one generic "inbox" badge.
- Apple Watch complication reuses the exact same color-coded left-bar-plus-name-plus-amount row pattern as the phone's category rows, just condensed to two sections ("Pinned", "Bills") — a deliberately reduced glanceable subset rather than a miniaturized full budget.
- Education content on Home ("YNAB Foundations", "Learn the Method", "4 min • Read") is woven directly into the primary Home feed rather than being tucked into a separate Help/Learn tab, alongside a direct human-support CTA ("Connect With Our Team") on the same screen.
- The Cover-Overspending category picker reuses the identical grouped-list-with-green-pill-amounts visual language as the main Budget table and the Top-Priorities list — one row/list component appears to be reused across at least four different contexts (Budget, Top Priorities, Goal picker, Together members), which reads as a single consistent "entity row" primitive throughout the app.
