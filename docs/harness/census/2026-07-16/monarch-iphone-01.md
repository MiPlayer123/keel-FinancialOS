## Business rules implied
- Category/merchant/group are three interchangeable slicing dimensions over the same underlying transaction set in a cash-flow report, with category as the default (`iphone-01`).
- A category row's percentage is computed as share of its section total (Income rows sum to 100% across just the income section; expense rows sum to ~100% across just the expense section), i.e., percentages are scoped per Income/Expenses, not against a combined total (`iphone-01`: Paychecks alone is "100.0%" of Income; seventeen expense categories each show a percent that sums toward 100% of Expenses).
- A negative-amount entry within an Expenses breakdown (a refund/credit landing in an expense category) is still listed under Expenses but rendered with the "positive/income" green treatment and a negative percentage, rather than being excluded or re-bucketed (`iphone-01`, "Entertainment & Recreation, -$10 (-0.3%)").
- Budget rollover/recalculation for the Expenses category is visually flagged with a refresh-style icon next to its "remaining" figure, implying expenses remaining amounts can be recomputed/rolled differently than a simple budget-minus-spent figure (`iphone-03`).
- A budget line's "remaining" can go negative when contributions/spend exceed the budgeted figure, and this is still displayed (not hidden or errored) — Goals row shows "$1,000 budget" against "$2,000 contributed" with a negative remaining value (`iphone-03`).
- Goal progress explicitly tracks four independent figures simultaneously — total saved, left to save, total spent, available to spend — meaning a goal can have both a savings balance and a separate spending allowance drawn against it (`iphone-06`).
- A goal's pacing status ("At risk") is evaluated against its target date and current progress rate, distinct from the raw percent-of-target figure — both are shown side by side rather than one implying the other (`iphone-06`).
- Net worth aggregation rolls up into named groups (Cash, Credit cards, ...) each with its own subtotal, its own period-over-period change (amount, percent, window), and its own "% of assets" share — group-level analytics exist independently of the top-level net-worth change stat (`iphone-07`).
- Every individual linked account displays a "last synced" relative timestamp directly under its balance, implying balances are pull/sync-based (not necessarily real-time) and freshness is a first-class piece of context users are meant to see alongside the number (`iphone-07`).
- Recurring payment items are modeled with a due-in-N-days countdown before they're paid and flip to a completed state carrying a specific paid-on date once settled, i.e., the underlying recurring/bill object has at least a status field (upcoming/complete) and a date field that changes meaning depending on status (`iphone-05` vs `iphone-08`).
- The dollar total "$59.42 remaining due" and its constituent Netflix ($22.99, in 10 days) / Disney+ ($15.99, in 15 days) rows are identical between the "Subscriptions" card (`iphone-05`) and the "Upcoming" card (`iphone-08`), suggesting "Subscriptions"/"Upcoming" reference the same underlying due-items query, just recomposed for two different marketing narratives (bills-vs-subscriptions grouping, and complete-vs-upcoming status grouping).

## Standout details
- Inline percentage-of-total shown parenthetically next to every dollar figure in the Cash Flow report ("$1,385 (41.1%)") — a lightweight way to convey proportion without a separate chart, reinforced by an actual proportional fill-bar behind the row.
- Sign-aware color coding that's about direction of money, not category identity: a refund inside an expense category still gets the "green/positive" treatment (`iphone-01`), and goal stat tiles use a "+" prefix plus green specifically for "money working in your favor" (saved, available) vs. plain black for neutral totals (`iphone-06`).
- Change/delta stats are never shown as a bare number — they're always packaged as [direction arrow] + [$ delta] + [(%)] + [time window label] in one glance, repeated verbatim across Accounts and Goal-Timeline screens (`iphone-07`, `iphone-06`).
- Per-account "last synced" freshness timestamp directly under every balance — a small trust-building affordance that answers "is this number current?" without the user having to ask (`iphone-07`).
- Two visually distinct chip styles for budget figures — an outlined/boxed chip for the editable budgeted amount vs. a filled colored pill for the computed remaining amount — encodes "this one you can change" vs. "this one is derived" purely through container styling (`iphone-03`).
- A refresh/rollover glyph placed inline with a single row's "remaining" figure (not the whole screen) to flag that just this figure is recalculated/rolled differently (`iphone-03`).
- Sparkle icon appearing only on the "Complete"/"Upcoming" bill cards and not on the plain "Bills"/"Subscriptions" cards — a small, unexplained visual cue that a smarter/AI-assisted detection process may be behind bill status tracking (unconfirmed by copy).
- Award/press citations are formatted consistently as bold claim + small gray "Source, Year" underneath (e.g., "Best Budgeting App" / "Wall Street Journal, 2025"; "Best Mint Replacement" / "Forbes 2025"), reused across multiple marketing screens as a trust motif.
- Dual-line percent-return benchmark chart (portfolio vs. S&P 500) against a dashed 0% baseline, letting a user see relative over/under-performance at a glance rather than just absolute portfolio value (`iphone-04`).
- Goal timeline chart mixes a solid (actual, stepped) area with a dashed (projected) line converging on a distinct target-marker glyph — visually separating "what happened" from "what's projected" within one chart (`iphone-06`).
