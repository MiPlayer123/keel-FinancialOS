## Business rules implied
- Net worth = Assets total + Liabilities total, and is surfaced identically on both Accounts ("$14,763.85") and Home ("Net position $14,763.85") — the same underlying figure reused across two surfaces (`accounts-desktop.png`, `dashboard-desktop.png`).
- Cash flow "Net" = In − Out, computed and displayed explicitly rather than left for the user to subtract ($11,983.76 − $7,557.22 = $4,426.54 shown as "Net $4,426.54") (`dashboard-desktop.png`).
- Transactions can carry a "PENDING" status distinct from posted, shown only on the 2 most-recent rows in the ledger, implying pending/posted is a real per-transaction field, not purely a function of recency past some number of rows (`ledger-desktop.png`).
- Transfers between two of the user's own accounts are matched and rendered as linked "Transfer ←/→ [account]" pairs in place of a category, with the outgoing leg red and the incoming leg black — implying a transfer-matching/reconciliation process runs over the ledger and suppresses double-counting them as ordinary income/expense category rows (`ledger-desktop.png`, "Payment Thank You-Mobile" +$671.35 / "Payment to Chase card ending in 8311" -$671.35 same amount, opposite signs, same day).
- Incoming person-to-person payments (Zelle/Venmo-style credits) are categorized "Other Income" regardless of the free-text note attached, even when that note describes food/drink (e.g. `Leo Li "Foooooood"` +$47.69 → "Other Income"; `David Kwon "drank"` +$2.71 → "Other Income"), while outgoing/debit transactions with similar merchant-style text get substance categories (e.g. "Food & Drink", "Shopping") — implying categorization keys off transaction direction/sign at least as much as (if not more than) memo text, and that raw ingested memo text is not itself driving category assignment for credits (`ledger-desktop.png`).
- The dashboard's "Biggest purchase" and "Top merchant this month" stat cards both surfaced the same transaction — a credit-card bill payment to Discover ("ONLINE PAYMENT TO DISCOVER CARDS 07/15", $895.33) — implying these two metrics' underlying queries do not exclude Loan Payments/transfer-like categories from what counts as a "purchase" or "merchant," a modeling gap rather than an intentional rule (`dashboard-desktop.png`).
- The ledger's aggregate footer ("167 transactions … In $49,940.34 Out -$60,042.97") reflects the entire filtered set even before the "Show 47 more of 47" expander is used — implying summary totals are computed server/query-side over the full result set, not client-side over only the rendered rows (`ledger-desktop.png`).
- A single very large outgoing transfer (-$25,000.00, "FID BKG SVC LLC MONEYLINE…", 05-20, categorized "Transfers") receives no distinct visual treatment (no confirmation flag, no "large transaction" badge) in the register itself, even though it is large enough to visibly move the Home net-worth chart into negative territory for roughly a month (`ledger-desktop.png` crop 09 vs. `dashboard-desktop.png` net-worth chart dip beginning ~05-20).

## Standout details
- Dates, merchant text, and amounts in the Ledger all render in a shared monospace/tabular typeface, distinct from the rest of the app's proportional sans-serif — gives the register a deliberate "raw ledger/terminal" feel that differentiates it from the softer card-based Home/Accounts pages.
- The Home "Net worth · last 90 days" chart fills green above zero and red below zero on the *same* line/area, encoding sign with color region rather than a separate up/down indicator — a compact way to show "currently underwater" at a glance.
- "Cash flow · last 30 days" leaves "Out" in plain black rather than red, reserving red specifically for the *net* negative/loss states (account balances, net-worth dips) rather than every outflow — a more restrained use of red than the Ledger, which does color every individual negative amount red.
- Account-type label directly under the account name (e.g. "Checking", "Credit Card") is used as adjacent context for every balance on both Accounts and Home, without exception — no bare balances appear anywhere in this unit.
- The Review page's empty state uses a dashed border (vs. every other card in the product being solid-bordered) — a deliberate "this container currently holds nothing" visual convention worth confirming is used consistently everywhere the app has empty states.
- Transfer rows in the Ledger swap out the normal editable category-dropdown affordance for a directional "⇄ Transfer ←/→ [account]" indicator — visually signaling "this row is system-linked, not a free category choice" without needing separate copy.
