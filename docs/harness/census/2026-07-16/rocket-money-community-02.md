## Business rules implied
- A transaction row can be excluded/hidden from budget calculations via a per-row toggle distinct from editing it (two separate icons: pencil vs. circle-slash), per `community-09-dashboard-recent-transactions-web.png`.
- Category assignment is per-transaction and user-editable at the row level (category icon shows a hover tooltip naming the category, e.g. "Home Improvement"), per `community-09-dashboard-recent-transactions-web.png`.
- Recurring charges due within a rolling 7-day window are aggregated into a single count+total statement ("6 recurring charges... for $377.30"), implying the system tracks a rolling look-ahead window and a running total of near-term obligations, per `community-09-dashboard-recent-transactions-web.png`.
- Cancellation is offered only for services the system recognizes as cancelable ("We've compiled a list of your services that we can cancel"), implying a curated/matched merchant-cancellation capability rather than blanket support for every recurring item, per `community-10-cancel-subscription-modal-web.png`.
- Budget progress is tracked per bucket type independently — "left to spend" (Spending, discretionary categories), "left to pay" (Bills & Utilities), and "to earn" (Earnings) are three distinct progress semantics on the same screen rather than one generic progress bar, per `community-x01-budget-view-mobile.png`.
- "Everything Else" functions as a catch-all aggregate category that itself contains multiple sub-categories (chip row + "+2"), implying budgets support nested/grouped categories, per `community-x02-budget-view-appstore.png`.
- Net worth is decomposed into exactly two top-level components, Assets and Debt, with Net Worth = Assets − Debt displayed as a derived third row (Assets $16.7k − Debt $5.11k = Net Worth $11.59k, consistent in both x03 and x04), per `community-x03-net-worth-summary-mobile.png` and `community-x04-net-worth-appstore.png`.
- Percent-change trend indicators are shown per line item (Assets, Debt, Net Worth) individually, not just at the top-line net-worth level, implying period-over-period deltas are computed and stored per component, per `community-x03-net-worth-summary-mobile.png`.

## Standout details
- The "Coming Up" panel folds count, time window, and dollar total into one plain-English sentence ("You have 6 recurring charges due within the next 7 days for $377.30.") instead of three separate stat tiles — a narrative-summary pattern worth studying for KEEL's typed-AI-response tldr requirement.
- Positive/credit amounts are distinguished purely by color (green) + explicit "+" sign, with no separate icon or label — a minimal, scannable convention for money-in vs. money-out in a dense table.
- The Shopping category ring icon overlays a small warning badge directly on the icon itself (not as separate text or a banner), keeping the over-budget signal adjacent to the number it qualifies without adding a row.
- "Frequently Asked Questions" is embedded as a collapsible row directly inside both the Budget and Net Worth screens (not off in a separate help section) — contextual help lives where the user is confused, one tap away.
- The cancel-subscription modal narrows a 13-item list down to a smaller curated, alphabetized, search-enabled list scoped specifically to "services we can cancel" — separating "everything we track" from "everything we can act on for you," which maps well to KEEL's suggest-vs-autonomous action boundary.
- Debt's "↑" trend arrow is colored orange/red while Assets' and Net Worth's "↑" arrows are green — same glyph, opposite sentiment coloring depending on whether "up" is good or bad for that specific line item.
