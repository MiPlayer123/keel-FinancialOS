- Dashboard is a vertically-scrolling stack of independent cards, in this observed order top-to-bottom: Net Worth → Recent Transactions → Top Trending Categories This Month → Top Payees This Month → Spending Over Time → Net Income by Month. Each card appears to be its own tappable module rather than a single unified report.
- Net Worth is one tap from the dashboard: tapping the Net Worth card's summary figure leads to a dedicated Net Worth detail screen (`iphone-16`) showing "As of Dec 20, 2024" with a back-chevron (‹) and an info icon (ⓘ) in the header — i.e., net worth has its own as-of timestamp and an explanatory affordance, one click from the dashboard.

- Investments/Holdings is a separate top-level surface (not visible how it's reached from the dashboard shown here — no Investments card appears among the dashboard cards captured) with its own header "Investments" and three peer tabs directly beneath the header: "Holdings" (selected in both captures), "Accounts", "Watch List". Holdings, Accounts, and Watch List are grouped together as siblings under one Investments surface.
- "Spending by Category" (`iphone-15`) is a report-style screen with its own header, distinct from the dashboard's "Top Trending Categories" card — it exposes a full set of date-range presets as one click, not available on the dashboard widget.
- Grouping within Net Worth detail: line items are clustered under implicit/explicit section headers — an unlabeled leading group (My Checking, My Savings, i.e., cash/bank accounts), then "OTHER ASSETS" (Car Value, My Home), then "INVESTMENTS" (401(k), Brokerage, Buy Hold, EFT Account, Family Brokerage, and more below the fold). ## Business rules implied
- A transaction has a mandatory in/out polarity captured as a first-class UI toggle ("Money In" / "Money Out"), not just inferred from sign of amount — evidenced by `iphone-13-3.png`.
- Cash balance is tracked separately from, and unaffected by, the mark-to-market swing of holdings: "Cash Balances: $9,634.50" is identical in both the up-day and down-day Investments captures (`iphone-09`, `iphone-11`), while the Market Value total and its change pill differ.
- Net worth is computed and displayed as an explicit as-of-date snapshot ("As of Dec 20, 2024"), not a live/undated figure — evidenced by `iphone-16-5.png`'s header.
- Net worth decomposes into Assets and Liabilities, and Liabilities are displayed as a negative figure ("-$347,361.74") — evidenced by `iphone-16-5.png`.
- Account grouping under Net Worth follows a fixed taxonomy with named subtotal bands ("OTHER ASSETS", "INVESTMENTS") whose displayed subtotal must equal the corresponding source-of-truth total elsewhere in the app — the "$4,493,950.16" INVESTMENTS subtotal in `iphone-16-5.png` matches the total Market Value shown in `iphone-11-QC_2048×2732_1.png`'s Holdings header ($4,493,950 / cents differ only due to rounding at $4,493,950.16 vs $4,493,950), evidencing a single shared investments total feeding two surfaces.
- "Uncategorized" spend is tracked and reportable as its own bucket alongside named categories (it appears as the #2 ranked bucket in `iphone-15`'s list, $7,138.99), implying categorization is not mandatory at transaction entry and uncategorized totals roll up visibly rather than being hidden.
- A transaction can be split into multiple categories — evidenced by the "Split" toggle control adjacent to the single "Category" field on `iphone-13-3.png` (turning it on presumably replaces the single category field with a multi-line split editor, not directly observed here).
- A transaction carries an explicit clearing/reconciliation status distinct from category or review flags — evidenced by the "Status: Uncleared" row plus the separate "Not Reviewed" row at the bottom of `iphone-13-3.png`, implying at least two independent status dimensions per transaction (bank-clearing status, and review/reviewed status).

## Standout details
- Group-header rows in the Net Worth account list double as inline subtotal rows (e.g., "OTHER ASSETS" ... "$661,332.00" on one line) rather than being a plain non-numeric section divider — saves a scroll/tap to see the rollup.
- The Spending-by-Category donut supports a tap-to-drill callout beneath the chart itself (colored dot + category name + total) before the user ever scrolls to the itemized list — a lightweight "hover/select" affordance carried into touch UI.
- The sort-criterion label doubles as a mini-breadcrumb: the gray divider bar text above both the Holdings list and the Category list always echoes the currently active sort/filter ("NAME (A-Z)", "MARKET VALUE DAY CHANGE (HIGH TO LOW)", "LAST 3 MONTHS") so the user never loses track of ordering context while scrolling a long list.
- Empty form fields on the transaction detail screen use the action verb itself as the placeholder ("Add Check Number", "Add Attachments") rather than a generic label + separate "add" affordance — one fewer visual element per empty row.
- The up/down day-change pill on Investments recolors both the number and the arrow glyph together (green+↑ vs red+↓) but the big headline dollar total also inherits that same color (green "$4,493,950" vs red "$4,375,394") — the summary figure itself, not just its delta, is tinted by the day's direction.
- The persistent "Last synced from Quicken Desktop … ago" footer is pinned to the Investments screen at all times (not just an initial load toast), continuously disclosing staleness of the linked/synced data.
