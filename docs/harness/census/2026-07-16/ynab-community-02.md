## Information architecture
- Left rail (visible in community-09 and community-x05) is a narrow, icon-only, dark-navy vertical bar with exactly four icons stacked top to bottom: a tree/leaf logo mark, a house-shaped icon (highlighted/active — this is the Budget screen), a bar-chart icon (Reports), and a bank/column building icon (Accounts). No text labels are visible; the rail appears to be in a collapsed/icon state at this viewport width.
- The Budget screen is the "home" surface reached via the house icon. Reports (Spending Trends, and by tab: Spending Breakdown, Net Worth, Income v Expense) is a sibling top-level section reached via the bar-chart icon, with its own internal tab strip.
- Within Budget, filter chips ("All", "Income", "Savings", "Fixed Monthly", "Day-to-Day", "Nonmonthly", "Sinking Funds", "Underfunded", "Overfunded", "Money Available", plus a circular funnel/filter icon) sit directly under the month header — these look like saved/quick views over the same category table, one click from the default "All" view.
- Category rows nest under a Category Group header row (e.g. "Salaries & Wages - Mark", "Immediate Obligations", "True Expenses", "Needs", "Credit Card Payments"); groups are collapsible via a chevron and carry their own checkbox and subtotal row.
- Clicking/selecting a single category (e.g. "Home Maintenance") opens a docked right-hand inspector panel in place — no navigation away from the budget table; the inspector contains "Available Balance" and an expandable "Target" sub-section.
- Configuring a Target appears to be a two-step flow: a "Target Type" picker (three named types, scrollable list, more below the fold) is one layer, and the resulting configured target (amount, cadence, progress ring) is shown back in the same inspector panel.
- Auto-Assign is reachable from a persistent pill/button next to the "Ready to Assign" / "All Money Assigned" figure at the top of the Budget screen; opening it reveals a panel of one-click preset amounts, and choosing certain presets/actions triggers a confirming modal dialog naming the number of affected categories.
- In Reports > Spending Trends, breadcrumb navigation ("All Groups > Frequent") lets the user drill from an aggregate stacked bar down into one category group, and clicking a bar segment within that opens a transaction-level modal for a single category ("Groceries") — three levels of depth reachable from one screen: totals → group → transactions, all without leaving Spending Trends.

## Flow steps
1. User views Budget table with Ready-to-Assign balance showing unallocated funds (e.g. "$8,650.00 Ready to Assign") → sees a green "Auto-Assign" button attached to that figure.
2. User clicks "Auto-Assign" → sees a dropdown of preset one-click actions with computed dollar amounts already filled in (e.g. "Assigned Last Month $2,550.00").
3. User selects an action that assigns to multiple categories at once → sees a confirmation modal: "Auto-Assign will automatically assign your money to your 23 expenses based on the due dates you have set." with Cancel / Assign Money.
4. User clicks "Assign Money" (not directly evidenced past this point — outcome screen not in this unit's images) or "Cancel" to back out, presumably returning to the unmodified table.
5. Separately, for Targets: user selects a category → inspector panel opens showing "Target ⌄" collapsed/expanded → user (inferred) clicks to change target type → a "Target Type" picker overlay appears with three named types to choose from → after selection, the inspector shows the configured target's plain-language description, a progress ring, and an "Assign $X to meet your target" call-to-action.
6. For Reports drill-down: user is on Spending Trends "All Groups" view → hovers/clicks a bar segment ("Frequent") → view updates to a "Frequent"-scoped chart with breadcrumb "All Groups > Frequent" and rescoped stat totals → user clicks further into a specific category segment ("Groceries") → a modal opens listing the underlying transactions for that category in the selected date range → user clicks "Close" to dismiss the modal and return to the drilled-in chart.

## States
- All-assigned / balanced state: "$0.00 / All Money Assigned" with a solid grey checkmark-circle icon (community-09, community-x05) — the neutral/success terminal state of budgeting for the month.
- Unassigned-funds state: "$8,650.00 / Ready to Assign" rendered in a green pill (community-x02) — the "money left to allocate" state, visually distinct (green vs. tan) from the balanced state.
- "Funded" state on a category: green word "Funded" plus a green (fully or partially filled) progress bar segment, and a green checkmark badge on the Available figure.
- Underfunded/needs-money state on a category: inline grey annotation text under the category name reading variants of "$X needed by [date]", "$X more needed by [date]", or "$X more needed this month" / "$X more needed" (no date), paired with an amber/gold clock-badged Available figure and a thin (often mostly-empty) progress bar.
- Disabled/inactive control state: "Redo" rendered in a visibly lighter/washed-out blue than "Undo", "Category Group", and "Recent Moves" — indicating no redo action is currently available.
- Modal confirmation state: exact copy "Auto-Assign will automatically assign your money to your 23 expenses based on the due dates you have set." — a business-rule-bearing confirmation that states scope (23 expenses) and basis (due dates) before a bulk write.
- Selected/active row state: a category row (e.g. "Home Maintenance") shown with a light lavender/purple background and a checked checkbox when selected, opening the matching detail panel on the right.
- Target progress state: circular ring literally labeled "0%" — a zero-progress state for a newly configured or not-yet-funded-this-period target, paired with an amber banner "Assign $50.00 to meet your target."
- No empty, loading, or error states are present anywhere in this unit's seven images — all captures show populated, successful states.

## Business rules implied
- Every dollar must be assigned for the month to reach the terminal "All Money Assigned" ($0.00, checkmark) state; a nonzero Ready-to-Assign balance is shown in a visually different (green, action-attached) treatment (community-09 vs. community-x02).
- Auto-Assign presets are computed from the account's own transaction history ("Assigned Last Month", "Spent Last Month", "Average Assigned", "Average Spent") and from current allocation state ("Underfunded", "Reduce Overfunding"), rather than being arbitrary manual entries (community-x01, right panel of community-x02).
- Bulk auto-assignment requires an explicit confirmation step that discloses the number of categories/expenses affected and the basis of the calculation ("based on the due dates you have set") before committing money (community-x02 modal).
- Categories tied to a scheduled/recurring transaction (payroll deductions in community-09; due-date-tracked bills in community-x02) get a distinguishing calendar/clock badge on their Available figure, separate from categories with no scheduled activity (plain grey pill).
- A category can be in a "Funded" state (target met for the period, green checkmark + green bar) or a "needs more" state (inline dollar-amount-and-date annotation + amber clock badge + partial/empty bar) — these are mutually exclusive, evidenced side by side in the same table (community-x05: Home Maintenance "needs" vs. Groceries "Funded").
- Credit Card Payments is treated as a structurally different category type from ordinary spending categories: its Available cell shows a "❓ PAYMENT" badge rather than a plain amount/checkmark/clock badge (community-x05), implying the system computes this figure differently (likely tied to card balance owed, not a manual target).
- Targets have at least three distinct configurable types with different accumulation semantics, each named and explained in-product: "Needed For Spending" (refill up to an amount, can spend from it), "Target Savings Balance" (save to a balance and replenish any spend-down), and "Monthly Savings Builder" (contribute a fixed amount every month indefinitely until disabled) (community-x06).
- Reports figures are recomputed and rescoped as the user drills down (All Groups → a single group → a single category's transactions), each level showing its own "Average Monthly Spending" / "Total Spending" totals scoped to that level, and a breadcrumb records the drill path (community-x03, community-x04).
- A budget can carry a free-text nickname distinct from the month label (e.g. "Budgetpalooza" shown under "JUL 2021") (community-x02).

## Standout details
- The inline "$X (more) needed by [date]" micro-copy directly under a category name is a compact way to surface both the shortfall amount and its deadline without a separate column or a progress-bar-only treatment — encodes urgency contextually right where the eye already is.
- Auto-Assign preset labels are phrased as verbs/nouns paired with a live dollar figure already computed ("Reduce Overfunding  $1,793.23"), so the user sees the effect before choosing to act — no blind action names.
- The "PAYMENT $30.00" badge with a "❓" icon on Credit Card Payments is a deliberately distinct visual/semantic treatment flagging that this number behaves unlike a normal category total — worth stealing as a pattern for "this figure is derived, not directly editable."
- Target Type picker uses a three-part copy formula per option: bold name, italicized "Use for: [examples]" (concrete use cases), then a plain description of the mechanic — this teaches the mental model of each option compactly without a separate help screen.
- The confirmation modal for Auto-Assign states the literal count of affected items ("23 expenses") and the rule basis ("due dates you have set") — turns an opaque bulk-write into an inspectable, specific commitment before it happens.
- Progress toward a target is shown twice, redundantly, in two forms in the same panel: a thin inline bar in the table row, and a circular ring + percentage in the detail inspector — reinforcing the same fact at both list and detail density.
- The Reports drill-down keeps the exact same chart/stat layout at every zoom level (All Groups → group → category-transactions modal) and only swaps the data + adds a breadcrumb, minimizing the user's re-orientation cost when going deeper.
- Payee names in the transaction modal are truncated with a trailing ellipsis at a fixed column width ("Green Gro…", "Farmer's M…") rather than wrapping or overflowing — a deliberate density choice for a compact popover table.
