## Business rules implied
- Net worth = Assets − Liabilities, computed and displayed consistently: $929,008.67 − $241,966.88 = $687,041.79 matches the hero figure exactly (`community-10`).
- Goal "Available for goals" total is the sum of each linked account's uncommitted balance (Total balance − sum of that account's goal allocations): Jon's Savings $30,000 − ($5,000 Vacation + $20,000 Emergency Fund) = $5,000 Available, and the four accounts' Available amounts ($5,000 + $5,000 + $2,000 + $2,000) sum to the panel-level $14,000 total (`community-13`).
- A single physical account can fund more than one goal simultaneously (Jon's Savings funds both "Vacation" and "Emergency Fund" as named sub-allocations) — goal funding is a soft allocation layer on top of real account balances, not a separate ledger/account per goal (`community-13`).
- Budget "Remaining" is not simply Budget − Actual at the leaf level for groups with rollover: Food & Dining shows Budget $1,462, Actual $1,531 (over by $69) yet Remaining is still positive, "$30" with a rollover icon — implying unspent budget from a prior period is carried forward and can absorb an overage in the current period (`community-15`).
- Budget line items distinguish "fully spent, nothing to roll over" (plain gray "$0" pill, Mortgage) from "unspent amount carries forward" (green "↻ $850" pill, Home Improvement) via icon + color, not just number sign — both are technically "$0 remaining this period vs positive remaining," but only one gets the rollover treatment shown (`community-15`).
