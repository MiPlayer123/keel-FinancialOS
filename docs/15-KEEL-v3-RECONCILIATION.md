# KEEL v3 — Reconciliation of Plan v1.2 and the External v2 Audit
*Written July 10, 2026, by the author of v1.2, having read the v2 audit in full. This document is the honest merge: what v2 got right and is hereby adopted, where it overcorrected, and the single decision that determines which plan governs.*

## 1. Ruling on the v2 audit overall
The v2 audit is high quality and its central criticism is **correct and accepted**: v1.2's "build-ready, ~27 agent-days" verdict conflated *code-complete* with *product-trustworthy*. The 27-day figure was an estimate of writing the deterministic system against fixtures; it silently excluded the work that makes finance software real — provider failure handling, replay, revision semantics, statement evidence, calibration, support operations, and months of live-data exceptions. v2's reframing of v1.2 as "strategy-complete / architecture-incomplete" is adopted. Doc 14's "build-ready" verdict is formally retracted and replaced by v2's staged proof gates.

## 2. Adopted from v2 (now product law, superseding v1.2 where they conflict)
1. **Focus/Ledger dual-mode over one truth model.** Better than v1.2's tier framing: it resolves the "anyone can use it" vs "Quicken-grade" tension architecturally instead of by feature gating. Adopted verbatim, including the design thesis line.
2. **Proof as a UX primitive.** v1.2 had audit logs and deterministic endpoints; v2 elevates provenance to the visible center (proof drawer, show-the-math, as-of/formula/scope on every material number, financial truth state). Adopted. This *is* the trust moat, made visible.
3. **The backend gaps list (B-001…B-067) in its entirety.** Specifically conceded as real v1.2 omissions: immutable raw event store + replay sandbox; pending→posted supersession lineage (my status enum was insufficient); **journal revisions/reversals — v2 is right that "undo from audit log" was underspecified; an audit log is not undo**; statement/reconciliation evidence tables; identity graphs with reversible canonicalization; transactional outbox; operator repair console with typed corrections; AI decision records with calibration; backup/restore drills. These merge into TECH-SPEC as §2.5 (schema deltas) and are Stage-1 scope.
4. **AI risk ladder (A/B/C/D action classes).** Cleaner formalization of v1.2's autonomy policies. Adopted, including: confidence routes within a class, never across classes; class D (money movement, filing, payroll execution, trades, personalized directives) disabled at launch.
5. **Staged proof gates replacing the milestone calendar** — prototype → spine proof → private alpha → trustworthy beta → category V1, each with falsifiable exit evidence and kill criteria. Adopted as the *company* schedule (see §4 for the fork).
6. **Regulatory grounding**: CFPB 1033 in flux (architect for provider plurality, not a clean open-banking future); FTC Safeguards as the security benchmark; SEC robo-adviser line respected via the advice boundary; NIST AI RMF as the governance loop. Adopted.
7. **Pricing correction**: v1.2's $99/$249 was anchored on consumer budgeting comps; the Owner tier at ~$29–59/mo and a service-backed Pro tier better matches COGS reality and WTP. Adopted as hypothesis to test, not as settled pricing.
8. **Migration as the go-to-market campaign** ("Keep the power and history of Quicken…"), the design-partner cohort structure, false-positive signal list, and kill/pivot conditions. Adopted.

## 3. Pushback — where v2 overcorrects (three points, argued not asserted)
1. **Deferring the entire investment/tax/planning depth risks the wedge's own promise.** v2 defers lots/corporate actions to "24–36 month power parity" and tax/retirement engines to post-evidence. But the target persona is defined by Quicken-grade expectations, and v2's own red team lists "Monarch plus tags is good enough" as a kill attack — depth is the counter to that attack. Resolution adopted here: *earn-in, not defer-out*. Holdings/allocation/completeness-states ship early (v2 agrees); the lot engine and tax-position estimator become **the first post-alpha earn-ins, sequenced by design-partner composition** (if the first 20 partners skew investment-heavy, lots jump the queue ahead of receipts polish). What v1.2 got right and keeps: schema headroom for lots/tax from day one so earn-in is additive, not a rewrite.
2. **The timeline truth is between the two documents.** v2's 12–18-month category V1 is calibrated for a conventional senior team; it under-weights what agentic development actually compresses (the deterministic code, fixtures, test harnesses, importers — i.e., most of Stage 1's raw engineering). v1.2 under-weighted everything else. The reconciled position: **agent-days compress code, calendar months are consumed by live data, partners, and trust** — the spine proof can plausibly land in 3–4 weeks of agent-driven work rather than 6–8 of team work, but alpha→beta→V1 gates are clock-bound by exception discovery and human validation, and v2's gate structure governs those regardless of build speed.
3. **v2 quietly assumes the company frame.** Staffing plans, kill criteria, pricing, design partners — all presume the goal is a funded company on day one. That was never decided (see §4). Its analysis is right *within that frame*; it is not a refutation of the personal-build path, which has different economics (no support burden, no trust barrier, no free-tier denial-of-wallet) and which v1.2 was originally scoped for before ambition inflated it.

## 4. The governing decision (the founder's, not the auditors')
Both documents now describe the same product with the same laws. The fork is intent:
- **Path A — Personal instrument first.** Build for one household (yours): prototype → spine → your own data → your own monthly closes. v1.2's agent-day framing is roughly honest *here*, minus its "build-ready" hubris; v2's Stage 0–1 content still governs what gets built first. No pricing, no partners, no kill criteria — just the best financial cockpit one person owns. Option value: it becomes Path B's working prototype if pull appears.
- **Path B — Company.** v2's plan governs in full: 10-day prototype, design-partner recruitment, staged gates, kill criteria, staffing, fractional controller/CPA before the schema is treated as authoritative.
The two paths share ~90% of Stage 0–1 work. The only *wrong* move is executing Path B's obligations with Path A's commitment, or vice versa.

## 5. Feature completeness verdict (answering "do we have everything?")
Yes — with v2's additions the universe is now closed. v2 added no missing *financial* features to v1.2's taxonomy (its 232-capability universe is the v1.2 feature set decomposed to finer grain, which itself validates the research); what it genuinely added is the **operational/trust capability class** (raw events, revisions, operator plane, calibration, drills) and two product-visible features v1.2 lacked: the **Trust Center** and the **proof drawer / financial-truth state**. Both are adopted into the feature universe. Nothing from the v1.2 taxonomy was lost — deferred items remain in the horizon with earn-in triggers.
